## Brand & Style

This design system powers an Indian-first student and young professional co-living discovery ecosystem. Built to eliminate the friction, anxiety, and opacity of metropolitan accommodation hunting in hubs like Bengaluru, Pune, Gurugram, Delhi NCR, and Mumbai, the aesthetic bridges youthful optimism with institutional safety and verification rigor.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Clean Modern Geometric Humanism** layered with subtle **Vibrant Tech Glassmorphism**. The visual voice feels energetic, metropolitan, and safe: crisp white canvases, slate foundational surfaces, and precision cobalt-to-violet energetic accents that mirror the illuminated identity mark.

### Audience & Sentiment
- **Primary Audience:** Undergraduate and postgraduate college students, tech interns, entry-level engineers, founders, and migrating professionals aged 18–29 seeking PGs, shared flats, verified flatmates, and private rooms.
- **Secondary Audience:** Independent property owners, co-living operators, and administrative safety moderators.
- **Core Emotional Arc:** Warm welcome, utmost safety/trust, cultural resonance with contemporary urban India, zero visual intimidation, and frictionless social connection.

### Negative Design Constraints
- Never display commercial transaction pricing, rent numbers (e.g., ₹/month), security deposit negotiations, or subscription tier paywalls on public surfaces. Focus purely on lifestyle fit, verification credentials, safety scores, amenities, roommate compatibility, and spatial configurations.

## Colors

The palette balances clean utility with the vivid energy of urban Indian youth. High-contrast deep slate provides legible foundations, while electric cobalt and violet gradients provide dynamic focal points matching the brand mark.

### Primary & Accent Palette
- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`):** Deep Electric Cobalt Blue. Drives primary actions, active states, verified badge fills, and structural brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#7C3AED`):** Vibrant Violet. Signifies community, roommate chemistry, peer matches, and active interaction triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#00B4D8`):** Electric Cyan. Used as a high-visibility accent on badges, status dots, and subtle gradient stops.
- **Brand Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #1D4ED8 0%, #7C3AED 60%, #EC4899 100%)` for hero highlights, premium discovery moments, and empty state art.

### Neutral & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas / Background (`#F8FAFC`):** Slate 50. Clean, light, reduces glare on mobile screens.
- **Surface Elevation 0 (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure White. Used for content cards, sheets, and active form controls.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#F1F5F9`):** Slate 100. Structural groupings, inactive controls, badge fills, and subtle containers.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#E2E8F0`):** Slate 200. Structural hairline borders, divider strokes, and hover foundations.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep Charcoal / Slate 900. Accessible, crisp typography for titles and key data.
- **Text Secondary (`#475569`):** Slate 600. Body reading text, descriptions, and metadata.
- **Text Muted (`#94A3B8`):** Slate 400. Placeholder copy, icons in neutral states, secondary labels.

### Functional States
- **Success / Verified (`#10B981`):** Verified KYC badge, background-checked room, owner confirmed.
- **Warning / Pending (`#F59E0B`):** Profile incomplete, slot filling fast, pending visit confirmation.
- **Destructive / Error (`#EF4444`):** Flagged listing, blocked profile, missing mandatory verification.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans powers the entire typographic architecture. Its geometric balance, generous counters, and crisp x-height provide an approachable yet sophisticated tech-startup vernacular suited for both micro-UI tags and sweeping hero headers.

### Typographic Principles
- **Clarity Under Density:** Mobile accommodation cards display multi-line amenity metadata, badges, and locality information simultaneously. Generous line heights ensure readability in quick-scan situations.
- **Hierarchy Anchoring:** Large headlines emphasize space availability and community vibe without visual clutter.
- **Label Precision:** Accommodation tags (`PG`, `1 BHK`, `Shared Room`) and role markers use explicit medium and semi-bold weights with slight tracking expansion (`+0.02em`) to guarantee legibility at small scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts seamlessly from high-density mobile viewports (where Indian youth primarily browse listings) to expansive desktop management portals.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Vertical rhythm prioritizes single-column full-width cards with horizontal snap-scroll rails for tags and roommate previews.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Two-column grid configurations for listings and roommate cards.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at `1280px` canvas with `2.5rem` margins and `2rem` gutters. Three-column grid for space discovery; split-screen map & list layouts.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing operates on a strict 4px/8px incremental base rhythm. Component padding scales predictably: dense chips utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while comprehensive space detail cards utilize `space-lg` to create breathing room between identity credentials and room configurations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean atmospheric layering and low-contrast surface separations rather than heavy, dirty drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, structural floor.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` with border `1px solid #E2E8F0` and shadow `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Used for accommodation list items, verification checklist items, and profile cards.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive):** `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Used on active listing cards, floating filter panels, and dropdown menus.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Role Switchers & Sheets):** Backdrop blur `12px` with surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, and shadow `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Glass Accents:** Frosted overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over photo carousels to host badges (`Verified PG`, `Female-only Flat`, `Student Verified`).

## Shapes

The geometric shape philosophy uses warm rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`, where base radius is `0.5rem` / 8px). This creates an inviting, accessible environment that reduces the friction and intimidation typically associated with rental paperwork and lease hunting.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Micro Elements (Checkboxes, Tooltips):** `rounded` (4px / `0.25rem`)
- **Inputs, Buttons, Default Cards:** `rounded-md` to `rounded-lg` (8px to 12px)
- **Modals, Flyouts, Large Listing Cards:** `rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl` (16px to 24px)
- **Role Pills, Badges, Status Indicators:** `rounded-full` (9999px) for friendly tactile pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid electric cobalt `#1D4ED8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius, font `label-lg`. Subtly lifts with a violet-tinted glow on hover.
- **Secondary / Community Action:** Violet `#7C3AED` tint (`#F5F3FF` background, `#7C3AED` label, border `1px solid #DDD6FE`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, slate `#475569` text, background hover `#F1F5F9`.
- **Icon Button:** 40x40px square with `0.5rem` radius, centered icon, subtle border `#E2E8F0`.

### Indian Accommodation Type Badges
- **PG (Paying Guest):** Slate-blue pill (`#EFF6FF` background, `#1D4ED8` text, `#BFDBFE` stroke).
- **1 BHK / 2 BHK / 1 RK:** Warm slate pill (`#F8FAFC` background, `#334155` text, `#CBD5E1` stroke).
- **Shared Room:** Soft teal tint (`#ECFEFF` background, `#0E7490` text, `#A5F3FC` stroke).
- **Format:** Always accompanied by a 12px geometric micro-icon (bed, door, building).

### Role Pills
- **Tenant:** Deep cobalt pill with verified checkmark (`#DBEAFE` background, `#1E40AF` text).
- **Owner:** Indigo pill with property emblem (`#EEF2FF` background, `#4338CA` text).
- **Admin:** Neutral obsidian pill (`#0F172A` background, `#F8FAFC` text).
- **Shared Architecture:** Height 28px, full rounded (`9999px`), horizontal padding `12px`, typography `label-sm`.

### Room & Flat Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white card container with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Upper half: 16:9 media preview with pill tags docked on the upper-left (e.g., `Verified PG`, `No Brokerage`).
- **Information Architecture:** Property Title (`title-md`), Locality & Landmark (`body-sm`), Walking distance to metro/tech park/college (`label-sm`), Lifestyle tags (Wi-Fi, Food Included, AC, Power Backup).
- **Strict Prohibition:** Cards never show monthly rent amounts or security deposit values. The primary call-to-action focuses on "Explore Space," "Meet Flatmates," or "Schedule Visit."

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text Input / Select:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `12px 16px`, corner radius `8px`. Focused state: border `#1D4ED8`, ring `3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`.
- **Search & Metro Filter Bar:** Integrated search pill with dual inputs (Locality + Accommodation Type), search button with electric cobalt background.

### Verification & Trust Badges
- **Student / Pro Verified:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, emerald shield icon.
- **Safety Score Indicator:** Circular progress tag displaying safety metrics (verified locks, gated community, female-friendly).

### Modals & Drawers
- Centered modal on desktop with smooth scale-up transition; bottom sheet drawer on mobile viewports.
- Header with clear breadcrumbs, explicit dismiss target, and frosted surface backdrop.
